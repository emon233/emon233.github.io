01 · About

From admin panels to on-chain backends.

I'm a full-stack developer who started out building Laravel apps for businesses: an events company site, an inventory and distribution system, and a journal platform with its own editorial admin. That meant a lot of the unglamorous work I enjoy: data models, admin panels, roles and forms.

These days I'm moving into Web3 backend work. I write Node.js and Express services that let a Laravel app generate wallets, read balances and send native coins and ERC-20 / TRC-20 tokens on EVM chains and TRON. On the side I fork and study other people's infrastructure (bridges, indexer nodes, chain registries) to learn how it's built.

I'm currently with Grow Tech, based in Khulna, Bangladesh.
